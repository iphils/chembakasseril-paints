About Us

A name Odakkali trusts

Chembakasseril Paints is a locally owned paint and hardware store in Odakkali, proudly run by Mr. Benoy Issac. For years, we've helped homeowners, contractors and businesses in the area find the right paints and supplies for every project — big or small.

We believe in honest advice, quality products and friendly service. Whether you're touching up a single room or planning a full renovation, our team is here to help you get it right the first time.
